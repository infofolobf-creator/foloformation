# B2B Ideal Customer Profile: Coaching and Training Services

## Target Audience
- Enterprises in Burkina Faso

## Company Size
- Medium to large enterprises (50+ employees)

## Industry Focus
- Focus on sectors such as:
  - Agriculture
  - Mining
  - Telecommunications
  - Education
  - Government

## Key Characteristics
1. **Growth-Oriented:** Companies looking to scale their operations or improve efficiencies.
2. **Invests in Workforce Development:** Willing to allocate budget for employee training and development.
3. **Leadership Support:** Strong backing from company leadership for training initiatives.
4. **Change-Ready:** Open to adopting new strategies and methodologies.

## Pain Points
- Struggling with employee engagement and retention.
- Need for improve productivity and operational efficiency.
- Seeking competitive advantages through skilled workforce.

## Goals & Objectives
- Improve employee skills and knowledge.
- Increase overall organizational performance.
- Foster a culture of continuous learning.

## Buying Signals
- Requests for proposals (RFPs) related to training and development.
- Inquiries about coaching services or team workshops.
- Participation in industry conferences focused on workforce development.  

## Customer Journey
1. Awareness through marketing campaigns focused on training.
2. Consideration through free resources or workshops.
3. Decision-making facilitated by testimonials and case studies.

## Channels of Outreach
- LinkedIn
- Local business networks
- Industry conferences and seminars
- Direct outreach via email and phone

## Metrics for Success
- Increase in client engagement rates.
- Growth in training program enrollments.
- Positive feedback scores from training sessions.
